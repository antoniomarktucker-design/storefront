# Project context

This is the headless storefront for Antonio's premium UK pet store (Shopify back end, Hydrogen front end on Oxygen).
Read `docs/HANDOFF.md` first: it holds the strategy, decisions, product research and next steps.

Working rules:
- Be a critical partner, not a yes-man: push back on decisions that carry real risk.
- Never add fake reviews, fake urgency, invented specs or duplicate listings; Google Merchant Center and UK consumer law both apply.
- Product data and copy written by automations go to Shopify as drafts for approval, never straight to live.
- Prices, dimensions and delivery times stay as `[placeholders]` until confirmed with a supplier.
